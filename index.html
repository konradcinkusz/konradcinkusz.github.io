About Me

Software Engineer (M.Sc. Eng.) actively delivering commercial projects across fintech, retail, SaaS, and public-sector domains. Deep expertise in C#, .NET, and Azure; equally comfortable in greenfield microservices and large-scale legacy modernisation.

I integrate AI-assisted development tools (GitHub Copilot, LLM APIs) daily to accelerate delivery quality and velocity. Founded aurelius.today — an AI governance & workflow automation platform with a Multi-Agent Simulation Whiteboard, prompt version control, and a community marketplace. MVP complete with 3 frontend apps, 7 microservices, and production-ready Azure deployment.

Active researcher in LLM-powered Multi-Agent Systems at Warsaw University of Technology — the top-ranked polytechnic in Poland — with publications at IEEE/ACM and ICAART conferences. Author of the CogniSim platform — a simulation framework for AI-driven Agile project management.

Based in Madrid, Spain (EU citizen). Available for on-site roles and open to relocation within Europe.

Outside of engineering, I'm deeply passionate about mountain engineering — tackling demanding alpine routes and high-altitude treks — as well as competitive squash and precision archery. I also love train travel — I've crossed the United States coast-to-coast 4 times by Amtrak, experiencing the country's vast landscapes from the rails. I also genuinely love reading books, especially literature that expands perspective and critical thinking. These pursuits shape my discipline, focus, and resilience both on and off the keyboard.
